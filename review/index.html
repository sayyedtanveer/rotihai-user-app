Review data: Native screenshots run the real application screens in an Android emulator. Public kitchens, categories, dishes and photos come from a read-only catalog snapshot; accounts, orders and payment states are local samples. Web comparison captures use the deployed UI with intercepted sample responses. No live customers, orders, subscriptions or payments were created. State differences are intentional; this is a design comparison, not a pixel-diff or live checkout certification.
Browse by food type

Real sections and combo labels, collapsible groups, a section picker and focused section browsing replace the endless flat menu.

Availability stays clear

Inactive and closed kitchens remain visible in their categories. Unavailable ordering actions are disabled and existing basket items are kept for review.

Photos stay truthful

Missing or failed product images collapse to a clean text layout. Only authentic supplied photos are rendered.

One visual language

Bundled Inter typography, compact headers, orange/gold promotions, consistent forms and a persistent cart connect the customer screens.
